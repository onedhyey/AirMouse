- **Exp 5** — timing: 25 Hz sampling, delivered as 20-sample batches every 0.81 s (fixed). Start command byte 2 is a sensor mask (`01` = accelerometer only). The rate byte (0x01/0x19/0x32) and other bytes didn't change the timing. Logs: `logs/exp-start-*.log`.
- **Exp 6** — periodic re-start/flush writes (02, 03+02, …) every 100–200 ms: they either kill the stream or keep the 0.81 s cadence. Batching can't be overridden from the host. Logs: `logs/exp-flush-*.log`.
- Design consequence: tilt-*angle-change* → cursor (position-like, no overshoot), with batches replayed at 25 Hz. Added `run` and `calibrate` modes (`Sources/Motion.swift`).
- **Exp 7** — free-form button presses: 7× `EVENT 04` (BUTTON_PRESSED) at irregular gaps, fewer than the presses made. No payload, so the button can't be identified.

  - Accelerometer streamed steadily at 25.0 samples/s for 67 min.

- **Exp 13 — first hands-on test (uncalibrated)**: verified `action left` (1 press) and `action right` (2 presses) in real use. The user said it was "too sensitive". Tuned in config.json (no rebuild): gain 1100→800, acceleration 30→5, smoothing 0.35→0.25, deadband 0.0015→0.003. For fast moves that's about 45% of the old speed. Theoretical: a ±50° twist sweeps the full screen width.
- **Exp 14 — latency, smoothness, tremor** (user: the earlier speed was better, but it lagged, wasn't smooth, and picked up tremor).
  - The pipeline added its own ~0.8 s lag (each batch replayed at real time over 0.8 s). It now replays each batch in `playbackSeconds` = 0.35 s, so new motion is shown ~0.45 s sooner. Floor: the watch's 0–0.8 s batching (avg 0.4 s).
  - Output glides at 100 Hz (`glide` 0.35 per 10 ms) instead of 25 jumps/s.
